Placing Stop Losses In Buy Daytrades?
在买入日交易中设置止损？
Whatever you use as the initial Stop loss - Do Not Rush to move it.
不管你用什么作为最初的止损点，不要急着移动它。
I have utilized the ES (E-mini S&P 500) as an example to explain this topic.
However, please note that when applying these points to your own trading strategy,
make necessary adjustments based on the instruments and assets you use in yourtrades.
我利用ES (E-mini 标准普尔500)作为例子来解释这个话题。但是，请注意，在将这些
要点应用于您自己的交易策略时，请根据您在交易中使用的工具和资产进行必要的调
整。
If you are Trading the CBDR overlap with PDA - Stop is 15 points (ES/SP500)
under. If you are Trading a Run under the Asian Range - Stop is 20 (ES/SP500) points
under. If you are Trading ANY Sell Stop Raid - 15 points (ES/SP500) under the low/
entry.
如果你交易的是CBDR和PDA的重叠，止损点在15点(东SP500)以下。如果你在亚洲范
围内交易，止损点在20点以下(SP500)。如果你正在交易任何止损Raid - 15点
(ES/SP500)以下的低点/进场。
If you are Trading the 1st retracement into +OB - 5 points (ES/SP500) under LOD.
If you are Trading 2nd return for Sell Stops - 15 points (ES/SP500) under the LOD .
如果你在LOD下交易第一个回撤至+OB - 5点(ES/SP500)。如果你交易的是第二个回
报，卖出止损LOD下15点(SP500)。
If you are Trading any other setup not described above - use 50% ADR of the last 5
days subtracted from the Asian Range Low.
如果你交易的是上面没有描述的其他设置，使用过去5天的50% ADR从亚洲范围低点
减去。
