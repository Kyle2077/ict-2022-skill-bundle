Before a Bearish Breaker enters the market, liquidity is taken from the previous
trend element. If the trend is down, then the momentum occurs at the LH level, and if
the trend changes from rising to falling, then the momentum occurs at the HH level.
以前看跌者进入市场，流动性取自之前的趋势元素。如果趋势是下降的，那么动量出
现在LH级别，如果趋势从上升变为下降，那么动量出现在HH级别。
To confirm a bearish breaker, you need to wait for a change in the direction of price
要确认看跌突破，你需要等待价格方向的变化
movement and an impulse breakout of the order block.
订单块的移动和脉冲突破。
Let’s think why breaker blocks work:
让我们想一想断路器分块工作的原因:
First, a major player knocks out stop-losses below a low to take a position. The
buyers placed their stops there. Which need to be picked up. That is, a large player
opens a sell position before this, just in the place where we will have a Breaker Block,
initially it was OB in a downward direction.
首先，一个主要的玩家在低点以下敲掉止损，建仓。买家把他们的止损点放在那里。
需要捡起来。也就是说，一个大玩家在此之前开仓卖出，就在我们将有一个断路器块
的地方，最初是向下的OB。
