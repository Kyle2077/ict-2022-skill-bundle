*FVG (Fair Value Gaps) is a candle in which there is an impulse, where an
insufficiently large volume is traded with a lack of liquidity. This caused a sharp
momentum without consolidations.
*FVG(公允价值差距)是一个有推动力的蜡烛，在缺乏流动性的情况下交易量不够大。
这造成了没有整合的强劲势头。
An impulse movement can be triggered by large orders of one big player. The market
maker seized all the liquidity, causing it to be scarce for most other players. Since a
certain number of orders within the imbalance of a major player remains unfilled, the
market returns and closes this zone.
一个大玩家的大订单可以触发一个冲动动作。做市商攫取了所有的流动性，导致流动
性对于大多数其他参与者来说是稀缺的。由于在主要参与者的不平衡状态下，一定数
量的订单仍然没有完成，市场返回并关闭这个区域。
Fair Value Gaps (FVGs) can be found on all timeframes and there are numerous
trading strategies that can be implemented using them. In this example, we will
focus on utilizing FVGs for trend continuation after a significant impulsive move.
As previously mentioned, we can anticipate the price to return to these FVGs before
continuing in the same direction as the impulse move. Traders can take advantage of
this by waiting for the price to reach the FVG area of interest and then entering a trade,
targeting trend continuation.
公允价值差距(fvg)可以在所有时间框架中找到，并且有许多交易策略可以使用它们来
实施。在本例中，我们将重点关注在重大脉冲波动后利用FVGs进行趋势延续。如前所
述，我们可以预测价格在继续沿着与冲动相同的方向移动之前回到这些fvg。交易者可
以利用这一点，等待价格到达FVG感兴趣的区域，然后开始交易，目标是趋势延续。
