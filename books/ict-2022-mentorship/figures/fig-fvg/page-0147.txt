Understanding the Three-Candle Formation
理解三根蜡烛的形成
1.
Candle 1: This candle represents the high of the fair value gap. It is important to
note that the low of this candle will be the starting point for the fair value gap.
蜡烛线1:这根蜡烛线代表公允价值差距的高点。需要注意的是，这根蜡烛的低
点将是公允价值差距的起点。
2. Candle 2: This candle is where the fair value gap is formed. It is the candle that
separates the high and low of the fair value gap. Traders should pay attention
to the price action within this candle.
蜡烛2:这根蜡烛就是公允价值差距形成的地方。是蜡烛区分了公允价值差距的
高低。交易者应该注意蜡烛线内的价格变化。
