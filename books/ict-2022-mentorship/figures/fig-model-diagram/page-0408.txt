Remember, missing a move in the market doesn’t result in a losing trade unless
you use a flawed logic that leads to a losing trade. If today the setups we expected
didn’t form, but it doesn’t mean the market is against us. It’s just a missed opportunity
and dwelling on it won’t make any money or change the outcome. Instead, focus on
learning from the experience and developing a mindset that can handle setbacks.
记住，在市场中错过一个动作不会导致交易失败，除非你使用了错误的逻辑导致交易
失败。如果今天我们预期的设置没有形成，但这并不意味着市场对我们不利。这只是
一个错过的机会，停留在它不会赚任何钱或改变结果。相反，集中精力从经验中学习，
培养一种能够处理挫折的心态。
Trading is about studying, observing, and recognizing repeating patterns in price
action. It’s not about blindly following instructions to buy or sell. ICT teaching method
might not suit everyone, but it’s based on logical analysis of charts. Avoid toxic thinking
and negative self-talk. Accept missed opportunities as part of the learning process.
Stay positive, study, practice, and be prepared for the next opportunity.
交易是关于研究、观察和识别价格行为的重复模式。不是盲目的听从指令去买卖。
