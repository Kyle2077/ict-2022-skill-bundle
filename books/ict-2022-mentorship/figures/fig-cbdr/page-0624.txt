For a high probability London Normal Protraction sell day, the CBDR should be
less than 20 points (ES/SP500), and the Asian session range should be between 10
to 15 points (ES/SP500). The market can move one to two standard deviations of the
CBDR during the protractionary stage or Judas Swing.
对于高概率的伦敦正常延期卖出日，CBDR应小于20点(SP500东部时间)，亚洲时段
范围应在10至15点之间(SP500东部时间)。在持续阶段或犹大摆动期间，市场可以移
动一到两个CBDR的标准差。
