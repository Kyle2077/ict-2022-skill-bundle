Central Bank Dealers Range
中央银行交易商范围
The Central Banks Dealing Range occurs from 14:00 - 20:00 NY Time and it will
continue into Asian session.
中央银行的交易范围发生在纽约时间14:00 - 20:00，并将持续到亚洲市场。
By using 1h chart, mark the high and low of 14:00 – 20:00 NY Time (mark the
body of the candles and ignore the wicks).
通过使用1h图表，标记纽约时间14:00–20:00的高点和低点(标记蜡烛体，忽略灯
芯)。
Mark the middle of the range (sellers will be below the middle of the range and
buyers will be above it).
标记范围的中间(卖家将低于范围的中间，买家将高于范围的中间)。
Draw the deviations of above the range and below the range and extend horizontal
lines at every deviation.
画出高于范围和低于范围的偏差，并在每个偏差处延伸水平线。
For a sell trade expect a high at 1 deviation above the flout. After high is made, the
market will fall 2 deviations below the flout. Consider the high to form in London.
对于卖出交易，预期高点在1以上。在高点形成后，市场将下跌2个偏差。想想在伦敦形成
的高潮。
