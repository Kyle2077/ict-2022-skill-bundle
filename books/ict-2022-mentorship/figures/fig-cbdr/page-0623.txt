To put it simple, measure the number of points from the low to high of 14:00 –
20:00 NY Time range. For a sell trade, the high of the day will form at same number
of points above the 14:00 – 20:00 NY Time range (1 deviation). For example, if the
flout range is 15 points (ES/SP500), the high of the day will form at 15 points (ES/
SP500) above the flout. Target or low of the day will form at 15 times 2 below the flout
(2 deviations ), that will be 30 points (ES/SP500) below the 14:00 – 20:00 NY Time
range.
简单来说，测量14:00–20:00 NY时间范围内从低到高的点数。对于卖出交易，当天
的高点将在纽约时间范围14:00-20:00以上的相同点数形成(1个偏差)。例如，如果浮
动区间是15点(SP500 ),当天的高点将在浮动区间上方15点(SP500)。一天的目标或低
点将形成在15倍于2的低点(2个偏差)，这将比纽约时间范围14:00–20:00低30个点
(SP500时间)。
