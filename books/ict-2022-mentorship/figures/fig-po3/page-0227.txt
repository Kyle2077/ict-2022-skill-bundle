The next stage is always Expansion. This is not a pullback and not a reversal.
下一阶段总是膨胀。这不是回调，也不是逆转。
That is, after consolidation there is always an impulse movement.
也就是说，在盘整之后，总会有一个冲动运动。
We determine what kind of movement it is and in what direction it is. Once we
have an Expansion, we have a choice:
我们确定它是哪种运动，以及它的方向。一旦我们有了扩张，我们就有了选择:
•
there may be a Retracement pullback (we can return to the order block it just
left and then make another move up or down, depending on the direction in
which it moves).
可能有一个回撤拉回(我们可以返回到它刚刚离开的订单块，然后根据它移动的
方向再向上或向下移动一次)。
•
or maybe a Reversal.
或者也许是反转.
Once it reversed, there might be another Expansion move. It will then return to
Consolidation.
一旦它逆转，可能会有另一个扩展移动。然后，它将回到盘整状态。
***Again, Consolidation cannot be followed by Retracement or Reversal.
* * *再次，合并后面不能跟回撤或者反转.
Consolidation is always followed by Expansion. After Expansion comes
either Retracement or Reversal.
合并后面总是跟着膨胀。在...之后膨胀要么来回撤或者反转.
***The market does NOT do Consolidation -> Expansion -> Consolidation.
* * *市场不做合并->扩展->合并.
It does: Consolidation -> Expansion -> Retracement (before order block
and resumption of movement) OR Consolidation -> Expansion -> Reversal.
确实如此:合并->扩张->回撤(在订单阻塞和运动恢复之前)或合并->扩展->冲销。
