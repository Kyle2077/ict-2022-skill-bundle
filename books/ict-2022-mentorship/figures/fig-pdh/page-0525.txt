Bullish and Bearish Order Flow
看涨和看跌订单流
In a bullish order flow, buyers dominate the market, driving prices higher. As a
result, the market tends to seek buyside liquidity above the previous day’s highs.
Conversely, in a bearish order flow, sellers dominate the market, pushing prices lower.
The market then seeks sellside liquidity below the previous day’s lows.
