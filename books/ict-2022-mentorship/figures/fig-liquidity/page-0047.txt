Buyside and Sellside Liquidity
买方和卖方流动性
*Buy and Sell Side Liquidity (BSL/SSL) are areas of price in which buy stops
and or sell stops are mostly residing. If you can understand the higher time frame
perspectives and see where the “money” is, then you have a bias once you see price
moving off known areas of support or resistance. Price will seek the liquidity to either
reverse or continue in within its expansion move.
*买入和卖出方流动性(BSL/SSL)是买入止损和/或卖出止损主要存在的价格区域。如
果你能理解更高的时间框架观点，并看到“钱”在哪里，那么一旦你看到价格离开已知
的支撑或阻力区域，你就有偏见了。价格将寻求流动性逆转或继续其扩张运动。
