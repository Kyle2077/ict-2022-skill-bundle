The more liquidity accumulates above a significant price level, the more likely that
liquidity will be taken. Where price consistently bounces from the level of support or
the level of resistance several times, there is a huge number of stops of some players
and orders in the opposite direction of other players. It is important to focus on finding
such places, as you can find great entries after liquidity is collected. The more bounces,
the better.
在重要价格水平之上积累的流动性越多，就越有可能被吸收。当价格从支撑位或阻力
位持续反弹几次时，一些玩家有大量的止损，其他玩家有相反方向的订单。重点寻找
这样的地方很重要，因为你可以在流动性收集后找到很棒的交易机会。弹跳越多越
好。
