C.E. - Consequent Encroachment or “middle point” of any Gap or Inefficiency.
首席执行官。-任何差距或低效率的结果侵占或“中间点”。
The difference between two consecutive candles’ closing price and opening price is
called the gap. A gap occurs when prices skip between two trading periods, skipping
over certain prices. A gap creates a void on a price chart. Price gaps are simply areas
on the chart where no trading has taken place.
两根蜡烛的收盘价和开盘价之差称为差距。当价格在两个交易周期之间跳跃，跳过某
些价格时，就会出现缺口。一个缺口在价格图上造成一个空白。价格差距是图表上没
有交易发生的区域。

It is important to understand that the Up-gap acts as a support zone and the down
gap acts as a resistance zone.
重要的是要明白，上缺口作为支撑区，下缺口作为阻力区。
Why should we have NWOG/NDOG on our chart? Because institutions will want
to fill the gap before big move. As I mentioned above this kind of gaps are strong
support and resistance.
