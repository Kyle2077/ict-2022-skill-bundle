Immediate Rebalance
立即重新平衡
An immediate rebalance occurs when the market quickly adjusts to a new price
level, often after an imbalance or inefficiency in price action. It typically happens
withinashorttimeframe,suchaswithinafewcandles,andcanresultinarapidprice
movement.
当市场迅速适应一个新的价格水平时，通常是在价格行动失衡或效率低下之后，立即
出现再平衡。它通常发生在短时间内，如几根蜡烛，并可能导致快速的价格运动。
An immediate rebalance, as explained by ICT, occurs when the market reprices
backtoaspecificpricelevel,suchasaweeklygaplow,andthenquicklyfailstocontinue
inthat direction.This canbe seen whenthemarket movesupthrougha level,then
downthroughit,andattemptstotestitagainbutfailsimmediately.
正如ICT所解释的，当市场重新回到特定的价格水平，如周缺口低点，然后很快无法
继续朝着这个方向发展时，就会发生立即的再平衡。当市场向上通过一个水平，然后
向下通过这个水平，并试图再次测试它，但立即失败时，就可以看到这一点。
